How Predictive Analytics Works

  • The trained Random Forest model scores each employee with a leave probability.
  • High risk (≥75%) — strong attrition signal; retention action recommended.
  • Medium risk (55–74%) — monitor closely and address satisfaction drivers.
  • Low risk (<55%) — employee likely to stay.
  • Feature importance shows which factors (overtime, income, satisfaction, tenure) drive predictions.
